How to work a call
  • PHONES — Ringing lines flash red. Answer fast (goal: within 15 seconds). Check the ALI box: landlines show an address; cell calls only show an approximate location.
  • Use the question buttons to interview the caller. Always verify the address and callback number, then get the facts that matter for that call type (breathing, weapons, people inside…). Some calls have special actions, like CPR instructions.
  • CAD — Press Create Incident, pick the nature code, priority, and response zone. Then Assign units; CAD will recommend the closest ones. Priority 1 goal: dispatched within about 90 seconds of answering.
  • RADIO — Units call in status changes and requests. Acknowledge every transmission. Run plates in LEADS, call wreckers, send backup, re-tone volunteer departments that don't answer, and treat any EMERGENCY alert as your top priority.
  • Not every call needs units. Transfer calls that are outside your county, handle pocket dials and non-emergencies without dispatching, and link duplicate callers to the existing incident.
About the realism. The county agencies, fire department station numbers (100–2100), the Lawrence County EMS headquarters in Coal Grove, MARCS talkgroup names, and the area hospitals are real. Unit numbers, EMS post locations, callers, addresses, plates, and protocols are fictional or simplified for gameplay, and the map is schematic, not to scale. Never use this to make real emergency decisions.
